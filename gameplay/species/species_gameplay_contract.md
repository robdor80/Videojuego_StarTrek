# Species gameplay contract

## Purpose

Species data exists only where it changes gameplay: physiology, medicine, perception, communication, action resolution, cultural interpretation, or character creation.

It is not an encyclopedia and it is not a personality generator.

## Hard separation

SPECIES != CULTURE != CITIZENSHIP != FACTION != PERSONALITY

A character's runtime identity is built from:
- species physiology;
- individual biology;
- upbringing and culture;
- education and training;
- affiliations;
- personality;
- current physical/emotional state;
- knowledge and memories.

Species contributes capabilities and constraints, not destiny.

## Mechanical rule

Do not use flat fantasy-style racial bonuses.

Use contextual capabilities instead:
- enhanced strength modifies a relevant physical contest;
- antenna trauma can affect balance;
- telepathy unlocks an action only if the character actually has the capability and training;
- cultural familiarity changes interpretation of etiquette or insults;
- species-specific medical states change diagnosis and treatment.

Core resolves effects. AI presents them but cannot invent or grant them.

## Cultural rule

Cultural traits are priors, not compulsory scripts. Characters can adhere strongly, weakly, selectively, or reject norms entirely.

## Knowledge rule

Species/cultural information is exposed only when justified by training, experience, database access, medical clearance, prior contact, or current evidence. Private biological states do not leak merely because global lore knows them.
