# Habits, Wellbeing and Daily Life

Status: **FOUNDATION v0.1 — IN PROGRESS**

## Purpose

Represent how a character lives over days, weeks and months without reducing life to arcade-style happiness bars.

The system observes sustained patterns rather than treating individual leisure actions as stat consumables.

## Core principle

> **A good life does not grant superpowers. It helps the character remain near their normal professional capacity.**

Conversely, one bad night or one hard duty shift should not cause dramatic penalties. Sustained imbalance matters.

## Two layers

### Acute state

Short-horizon conditions such as:

- immediate sleepiness;
- immediate fatigue;
- hunger/thirst when relevant;
- temporary physical exertion;
- immediate stress response.

### Longitudinal habits

Patterns accumulated across longer windows:

- sleep duration and regularity;
- work/recovery balance;
- nutrition routine;
- exercise;
- leisure;
- social connection;
- intimacy;
- study load;
- repeated emergency duty;
- persistent conflict or isolation.

## Time windows

The default evaluation model observes overlapping windows:

- last 24 hours;
- last 7 days;
- last 30 days.

Different effects care about different windows.

A good night's sleep may improve acute fatigue substantially while only slightly improving a month of accumulated overload.

## Personal preferences

Recovery is not universal.

Different characters may recover best through:

- solitude;
- social time;
- exercise;
- reading;
- music;
- holodeck activity;
- creative hobbies;
- partner/intimacy;
- quiet rest.

The system should use persistent preferences rather than assuming that one leisure activity benefits everyone equally.

## No action-as-potion design

Forbidden model:

```text
Ten Forward = +10 happiness
Sex = +20 morale
Holodeck = +15 recovery
```

Preferred model:

```text
actual activity
+ character preference
+ social/relationship context
+ duration
+ recent workload
+ accumulated pattern
→ contribution to recovery / stress / routine
```

## Player agency

Fatigue, stress or irritability must not force dialogue choices or seize control of the player character.

The player decides what Roberto says and does.

Character state may influence:

- assistance available from the avatar's professional competence;
- physical endurance;
- sustained task handling;
- learning efficiency;
- recovery;
- NPC observations/reactions.

## Operational translation

The player human does not become more attentive because the avatar slept well.

Therefore wellbeing effects should primarily modify **character-side assistance**, not world truth.

Example:

A rested, skilled sensor officer may briefly highlight one particularly appropriate scan configuration.

A severely fatigued officer may receive no such cognitive assistance.

The sensor signal itself is unchanged.

## NPCs

NPCs use the same conceptual system.

They are not 24/7 services.

NPCs may be:

- on duty;
- off duty;
- eating;
- exercising;
- studying;
- socializing;
- sleeping;
- recovering;
- spending time with a partner.

## Long-term routine

Repeated real behavior can form observed habits:

- breakfast before Alpha Shift;
- gym three times a week;
- Ten Forward after final watch;
- Astrometrics study in the evening;
- regular dinner with a partner.

These are not selected perks.

They emerge from repeated activity.

## Routine disruption

Transfer, crisis, schedule change, relationship change or injury can disrupt a routine and alter recovery/social patterns.

## CoreRPG boundary

Generic actor state, fatigue, rest, longitudinal habits and availability are candidates for CoreRPG.

Star Trek supplies Starfleet duty context, holodecks, shipboard activities, quarters, Ten Forward-like social spaces and professional consequences.
