# Facility assignments and station gameplay

Stations, starbases, outposts and shipyards are playable duty environments.

A player may be assigned to a facility as:
- junior officer;
- specialist;
- watch officer;
- department head;
- executive/command staff where career state permits.

## Core idea

```text
FACILITY
→ departments
→ watches
→ local operational state
→ incidents / support requests / traffic
→ role-specific tasks
→ persistent consequences
```

A station assignment is not a waiting room for the next starship posting.

Examples of meaningful gameplay:
- coordinate incoming traffic;
- process docking clearance;
- investigate a security breach;
- handle medical overflow;
- prioritize scarce repair capacity;
- supervise engineering isolation after damage;
- manage cargo and personnel transfers;
- support visiting ships;
- coordinate patrols or search-and-rescue;
- participate in diplomacy or communications.

The same authority and knowledge rules used on ships apply here.
