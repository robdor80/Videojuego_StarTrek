# Starfleet Professional Life

Status: **FOUNDATION v0.1 — IN PROGRESS**

## Purpose

Model the player's working life after Academy as an actual Starfleet career rather than a sequence of disconnected missions.

## Professional life includes

- duty watches and relief;
- station/role assignments;
- supervision;
- qualifications;
- class familiarization;
- training;
- evaluations;
- commendations and reprimands;
- leave;
- temporary duties;
- transfers;
- promotion;
- mentorship;
- department responsibility;
- command pathways.

## Evidence first

Professional judgments must derive from actual evidence whenever possible.

```text
Operational Event Log
+ qualification history
+ service record
+ supervisor observations
+ mission outcomes
+ disciplinary record
→ professional assessment
```

AI may phrase the assessment. It does not manufacture the evidence.

## Duty

A player may:

- assume a watch;
- relieve another officer;
- receive orders;
- perform station duties;
- hand off unfinished work;
- file reports;
- request relief;
- be relieved by a superior or medical authority when justified.

Duty status is part of world state and timekeeping.

## Qualifications

Eligibility for a duty is not identical to rank.

Examples:

- Sensors Operator;
- Bridge Watch;
- Shuttle Pilot;
- EVA;
- Away Team Science;
- Damage Control;
- Command Watch.

A higher rank does not automatically grant every technical qualification.

## Mentorship and supervision

Junior officers may be assigned mentors or supervising officers.

Mentorship is a professional relationship that can coexist with:

- friendship;
- distance;
- conflict;
- high respect;
- low affinity.

Mentors can influence recommendations but cannot bypass formal requirements.

## Promotion

Promotion is not a level-up reward.

It may depend on:

- time in service;
- current rank;
- qualifications;
- performance evidence;
- conduct;
- responsibilities already demonstrated;
- recommendations;
- vacancies/organizational needs;
- campaign circumstances.

No single friendship or romance guarantees promotion.

## Transfers

The player may request a transfer or be considered for one.

A result may depend on:

- open billet;
- rank;
- specialty;
- qualifications;
- service record;
- needs of Starfleet;
- command recommendations;
- player preference.

Famous/canonical ships are never guaranteed merely because the player requests them.

## Professional reputation

Professional reputation is scoped.

Examples:

- department-level;
- ship command;
- local Starfleet unit;
- specialist community;
- Academy/instructor network.

It is separate from personal popularity.

## Life balance

Duty, training and career ambition consume time.

They therefore interact with:

- rest;
- relationships;
- leisure;
- study;
- wellbeing.

The game should allow the player to overcommit, but sustained poor self-management may become professionally visible.

## Long-term arc

The intended feeling is:

> **I earned this responsibility because of the career I actually lived.**
