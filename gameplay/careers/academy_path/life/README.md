# Academy Life & Social Simulation

This directory owns the Academy-specific assembly of shared character, population, schedule, wellbeing and social systems.

The 20-step execution plan is controlled by:
- `docs/roadmap/academy_life_social_simulation_plan_v0.1.md`
- `docs/roadmap/academy_life_social_simulation_status_v0.1.json`

Current closure: **Steps 1–7 COMPLETE — population + procedural cadets + academic grouping + quarters/roommates + personal schedules + free time + extracurricular activities.**

Step 1 defines population. Step 2 materializes persistent cadets. Step 3 assigns academic groups. Step 4 assigns finite quarters. Step 5 creates feasible timetables. Step 6 plans ordinary free time. Step 7 adds persistent extracurricular entities, finite memberships and recurring commitments without auto-generating relationships or skill. Conferences/seminars/additional courses remain Step 8.
