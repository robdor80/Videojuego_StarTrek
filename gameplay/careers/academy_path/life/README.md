# Academy Life & Social Simulation

This directory owns the Academy-specific assembly of shared character, population, schedule, wellbeing and social systems.

The 20-step execution plan is controlled by:
- `docs/roadmap/academy_life_social_simulation_plan_v0.1.md`
- `docs/roadmap/academy_life_social_simulation_status_v0.1.json`

Current closure: **Steps 1–15 COMPLETE — population + procedural cadets + academic grouping + quarters/roommates + personal schedules + free time + extracurricular activities + academic enrichment + social life + romance/dating + NPC↔NPC relationships + mentors/instructors + wellbeing/balance + obligations/consequences + campus events.**

Step 1 defines population. Step 2 materializes persistent cadets. Step 3 assigns academic groups. Step 4 assigns finite quarters. Step 5 creates feasible timetables. Step 6 plans free time. Step 7 adds extracurriculars. Step 8 adds optional academic enrichment. Step 9 adds causal social opportunities, invitations, circles and attendance plans without auto-creating relationships or romance. Step 10 adds adult, heterosexual-only player↔NPC romance/dating with directional interest, explicit player agency, consent gates and physically feasible dates. Step 11 adds autonomous persistent NPC↔NPC relationship evolution from confirmed events, including friendship, conflict/reconciliation and canon-constrained adult heterosexual romance. Step 12 adds persistent instructors, tutoring and voluntary professional mentorship with finite capacity and strict authority boundaries. Step 13 connects actual Academy activity to individualized acute/weekly/monthly wellbeing and habit evidence without formal consequences. Step 14 adds causal obligation resolution, justified absence, remediation, recurrence and authority/proportionality-gated discipline. Step 15 adds causal persistent scheduled/emergent/crisis campus events, real participation/obligation candidates and authorized schedule-preemption candidates. Off-screen Academy life remains Step 16.
