# Academy Life & Social Simulation

This directory owns the Academy-specific assembly of shared character, population, schedule, wellbeing and social systems.

The 20-step execution plan is controlled by:
- `docs/roadmap/academy_life_social_simulation_plan_v0.1.md`
- `docs/roadmap/academy_life_social_simulation_status_v0.1.json`

Current closure: **Steps 1–8 COMPLETE — population + procedural cadets + academic grouping + quarters/roommates + personal schedules + free time + extracurricular activities + academic enrichment.**

Step 1 defines population. Step 2 materializes persistent cadets. Step 3 assigns academic groups. Step 4 assigns finite quarters. Step 5 creates feasible timetables. Step 6 plans ordinary free time. Step 7 adds persistent extracurriculars. Step 8 adds formal optional conferences, seminars and additional courses with real capacity, presenters, schedules, waitlists and completion-policy separation. Social life remains Step 9.
