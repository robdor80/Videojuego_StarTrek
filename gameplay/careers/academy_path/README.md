# Ruta jugable de la Academia

Starfleet Academy is the first full playable career hub.

## Player journey

```text
CANDIDATO
  ↓
ADMISIÓN
  ↓
CADETE
  ↓
AÑOS / SEMESTRES / EVALUACIONES
  ↓
ESPECIALIZACIÓN + EXPERIENCIA
  ↓
GRADUACIÓN
  ↓
PRIMER DESTINO
```

The exact admission and academic mechanics are still under research.

## Core gameplay pillars

- **Tiempo** — classes, appointments, training and free time compete for the player's day.
- **Competencia** — courses, simulations and practical work build capabilities.
- **Expediente** — grades, commendations, reprimands and evaluations persist.
- **Relaciones** — cadets and instructors remember the player.
- **Elecciones** — specialization and behavior shape career opportunities.
- **Consecuencias** — failure is not always game over; it may mean retakes, delayed progression, discipline or altered opportunities.
- **Era** — the same Academy concept is presented and configured differently for Pike, Kirk and TNG/DS9/Voyager.

## Separation of concerns

Lore answers:
> What is Starfleet Academy?

Gameplay answers:
> What can the player do there and what changes because of it?

Both layers are required.
