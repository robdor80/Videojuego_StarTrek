# Kobayashi Maru — Academy Capstone Design

Status: **FOUNDATION v0.1 — IN PROGRESS**

## Purpose

The Kobayashi Maru is a major late-Academy evaluation.

It is not a conventional mission with a hidden winning solution.

## Core rule

> **The scenario evaluates the cadet's response to an adverse/no-win situation, not whether the cadet found the designer's preferred answer.**

## Evaluation domains

Depending on role:

- judgment;
- communication;
- leadership;
- responsibility;
- ethical reasoning;
- risk management;
- consistency;
- use of crew/team;
- treatment of uncertainty;
- ability to continue functioning under pressure.

## Role-specific participation

### Command candidate
May command the simulated crew and own the major decisions.

### Sensors / Science
May be evaluated on:

- detection;
- reporting;
- prioritization;
- uncertainty;
- recommending options;
- continuing useful work under pressure.

### Engineering
May be evaluated on:

- damage response;
- prioritization;
- power/resource decisions;
- communicating capability limits.

### Conn / Navigation
May be evaluated on:

- maneuver execution;
- route/risk assessment;
- interpreting command intent;
- communicating constraints.

### Tactical / Security
May be evaluated on:

- threat assessment;
- weapons/defense discipline;
- escalation judgment;
- protection priorities.

### Operations / Medical
Equivalent branch-specific evidence applies.

## Adaptive pressure

The scenario may select pressure patterns informed by the cadet's known academic profile.

Examples:

- overly cautious cadet → situation where delay has consequences;
- impulsive cadet → situation where premature action has consequences;
- cadet who depends excessively on one information source → conflicting/incomplete information.

This must not become arbitrary cheating.

The scenario still obeys its internal simulated reality.

## No single correct answer

Opposing choices may both be defensible.

Evaluation asks:

- Did the cadet understand the information available?
- Did the cadet communicate clearly?
- Did the cadet act consistently with stated priorities?
- Did the cadet use the team?
- Did the cadet accept responsibility?
- Did the cadet adapt to new information?

## Reprogramming / unconventional action

The UI must not expose a gamified "cheat the test" button.

If a cadet genuinely discovers and uses a valid system path to alter the simulation, the Academy reacts to that real action and records it.

The consequence is part of the cadet's history.

## Persistence

The first formal attempt is the meaningful Academy event and should remain in the record.

Later recreations may exist as training/simulation but do not erase the original assessment.

## Placement

The scenario belongs to the late-Academy capstone window.

Exact trimester placement remains configurable until the full four-year master curriculum is scheduled.
