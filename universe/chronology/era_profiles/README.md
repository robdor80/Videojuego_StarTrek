# Era Profiles

Era profiles are temporal filters over the single Star Trek knowledge base. They do not duplicate characters, ships, technology or institutions.

## Current profiles

- `pike`
- `kirk`
- `tng_ds9_voyager`

## Important distinction

The year ranges in these profiles are **reference windows**, not mandatory story arcs. A Voyager-based campaign is not required to reproduce the seven-year television journey or return to Earth in 2378.

Each profile records an era ID, display name, continuity, reference window, primary screen scope, anchors, campaign rule and availability rule. More precise dates may override broad year-level windows later.
