## Non-playable gap

### 2294–2363

This period still exists historically but is not currently a campaign-start era. It may later support legacy transitions, historical summaries, NPC biographies, ship service histories and technology evolution.

## Critical rule

These dates describe **reference history**. If a campaign starts before a later canonical event, that later event is not guaranteed to occur. The television future remains research material, not a forced script.
