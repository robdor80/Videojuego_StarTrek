# STAR TREK — PLANET / ENVIRONMENT VISUAL BIBLE v0.1

**Status:** FOUNDATION APPROVED FOR PROCEDURAL TESTING
**Inherits:** STAR_TREK_GLOBAL_VISUAL_BIBLE_v0.1

## Principle

A planet image is the visual output of an actual planetary profile, not a generic “alien world” prompt.

Resolve:

`planet physics → atmosphere → geography → climate → biome/ecosystem → settlement/infrastructure → current weather/event state → camera`

## Physical context

Visuals should respect, where known:
- stellar light context;
- atmosphere/haze;
- gravity implications;
- hydrosphere/aridity;
- geology/topography;
- vegetation/life;
- seasonal/weather state.

## Ecology

Biomes should look causally related to climate, terrain and water.

Avoid:
- random Earth-biome collage;
- ubiquitous giant moons;
- glowing flora by default;
- impossible mixed climates without geographic/canonical cause.

## Civilization

Settlements/infrastructure derive from:
- technology;
- population;
- culture/organization;
- resources;
- environment;
- transport.

Do not add a city because the frame “needs interest”.

## State continuity

Rain, snow, dust, flooding, fire, battle damage and pollution must follow environmental/event state.

Returning later may show evolved state, not regenerated geography.
