# Mission lifecycle validation rules

1. Every active mission must have a valid issuing authority or explicit local/emergency basis.
2. Mission objectives operate on live World State and cannot invent required entities retroactively.
3. Status transitions are authoritative Core events.
4. Primary, optional and hidden objectives remain distinct.
5. Optional objectives never silently become mandatory.
6. A hidden objective requires a valid knowledge/authority reason.
7. Players may satisfy an objective through an unanticipated method if the resulting World State meets its success conditions.
8. A failed or partial mission remains a valid campaign outcome.
9. New tasking may amend, cancel or supersede a mission without erasing history.
10. The commanding officer retains tactical discretion unless a valid authority rule explicitly limits it.
11. Mission generation cannot schedule future canon as mandatory history.
12. Mission state, route state, ship state and political state must stay separate but cross-referenceable.
13. AI may brief, summarize and roleplay orders but cannot issue authoritative mission state without Core authorization.
