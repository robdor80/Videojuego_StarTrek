# Travel encounter and mission-generation validation rules

1. Encounter templates never instantiate World Truth by themselves.
2. Core must create concrete entities/state before AI presentation.
3. Quiet travel is valid; no minimum event count is required.
4. Event probability may depend on route context but must not guarantee hidden danger.
5. Optional diversions do not replace current orders automatically.
6. Player duty obligations may create consequences for ignoring an event without removing choice.
7. First-contact missions require a genuinely uncontacted civilization/contact in live state.
8. Political and tactical events use current campaign relations and borders.
9. No future canon event is scheduled merely because the date approaches.
10. Mission generation must check ship capability and career authority.
11. Hidden faction, motive, cloak, damage or life-sign truth remains knowledge-gated.
12. AI cannot create, resolve or persist encounters outside Core authorization.
13. Repeated templates should be suppressed enough to avoid mechanical repetition.
14. Mission success/failure is resolved from actions and state, not narrative preference.
15. Famous canon characters/ships cannot be mandatory to make a generated mission function.
