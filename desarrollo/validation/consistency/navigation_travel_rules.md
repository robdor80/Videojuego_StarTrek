# Navigation and warp-travel consistency rules

1. Natural-language orders never mutate movement state directly.
2. Destination resolution must fail or request clarification when ambiguity is material.
3. Warp factor is not a universal speed formula.
4. Effective segment speed comes from the ship/configuration propulsion resolver and live state.
5. ETA is normally a range, not false precision.
6. Travel is persisted segment by segment and can be interrupted.
7. Physical distance, political legality and tactical risk are separate dimensions.
8. A physically shortest route is not automatically the recommended route.
9. Unknown hazards contribute uncertainty; they are not treated as known-safe space.
10. Borders and treaty restrictions use current campaign political state.
11. Future canon border changes never auto-apply after campaign start.
12. A player may deliberately choose a known illegal or dangerous route; the game resolves consequences rather than silently preventing agency.
13. Damage, power loss or subspace changes can force speed reduction or drop-out.
14. Special traversal mechanisms use dedicated resolvers rather than ordinary warp distance rules.
15. AI may explain, warn and roleplay navigation personnel but cannot advance ship position or fabricate sensor knowledge.
