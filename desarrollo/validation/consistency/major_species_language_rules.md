# Major species and language consistency rules

1. Species, culture, citizenship, faction and personality remain separate.
2. No species profile imposes a mandatory personality or profession.
3. Biological capabilities are contextual and resolved by Core.
4. Telepathy never becomes omniscience or automatic lie detection.
5. Bajoran faith, Klingon honor, Ferengi commerce, Cardassian hierarchy and Romulan secrecy are cultural contexts, not universal character locks.
6. Trill joining is a character/medical state; previous-host information remains knowledge-gated.
7. Romulan and Vulcan shared ancestry does not make their physiology or mental disciplines interchangeable.
8. Universal translation can fail, be partial or lose metaphor/context.
9. Machine translation does not grant cultural familiarity.
10. AI cannot infer faction loyalty, private medical state, religious belief or hidden motives from species.
