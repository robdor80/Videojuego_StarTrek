# Political-space anchor validation

1. Spatial region identity and political control are separate.
2. A region anchor is a campaign-start initializer, not a scheduled future event.
3. After campaign start, later canonical anchors are ignored unless the live campaign independently reaches them.
4. Neutral zones and controlled borders carry gameplay rules even when exact polygon geometry is unresolved.
5. Claims, control, access policy and diplomatic relation are distinct fields.
6. Bajoran sovereignty must not be overwritten by Starfleet operation of Deep Space 9.
7. A peace treaty does not imply borderless travel.
8. Restricted entry produces warnings/consequences, not an invisible wall that removes player choice.
9. Approximate/reference geometry must stay marked as such.
10. If screen canon and an atlas disagree, screen evidence governs the gameplay fact while the map remains a reference hypothesis.
