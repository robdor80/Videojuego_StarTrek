# Operational-need mission-generation validation rules

1. Every generated mission must trace back to at least one live operational need.
2. Every operational need must trace back to live World State, a standing duty requirement, or a valid command policy.
3. Mission templates never create the factual incident they respond to.
4. NPC units are eligible responders and may be selected instead of the player.
5. Player proximity is only one selection factor.
6. Hard capability, legal-access and authority constraints cannot be overridden by scoring.
7. A need may remain unresolved when no suitable unit exists.
8. Needs may expire, escalate, merge, split or be superseded.
9. A mission can resolve one need while creating additional consequences or needs.
10. Mission generation cannot auto-schedule future screen canon after campaign start.
11. Hidden World Truth is not exposed during need derivation or candidate scoring.
12. AI may explain or dramatize authorized state but cannot create authoritative events, needs or tasking.
13. Famous canon ships/characters are never required for generated operational logic.
14. Quiet periods with few or no player missions are valid when World State does not justify tasking.
