# Docking, maintenance and support-selection rules

1. Docking permission, physical compatibility and service availability are separate checks.
2. Docking follows an operational sequence and may require holding/queueing.
3. Emergency status changes priority but does not erase physical safety.
4. Maintenance state is separate from combat/accident damage.
5. Deferred maintenance changes risk; it does not guarantee scripted failure.
6. Repair requirements specify the minimum repair level actually needed.
7. Facilities below that level cannot perform full repair.
8. Stabilization and full repair are separate outcomes.
9. Support destination selection considers ETA plus queue, access, risk and capability.
10. Nearest facility is not automatically the best facility.
11. Damaged-ship travel limitations constrain reachable support.
12. AI cannot invent clearance, berths, parts, queues or completed services.
