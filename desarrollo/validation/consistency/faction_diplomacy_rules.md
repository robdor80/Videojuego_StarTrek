# Faction diplomacy validation rules

1. Faction != species.
2. Citizenship != faction office.
3. Uniform/service membership != authority to bind a government.
4. Relation state must resolve by date before campaign start.
5. Future canonical relation changes never auto-apply after campaign start.
6. Bilateral relation != personal attitude.
7. Border state must be checked independently from diplomatic friendliness.
8. Treaty existence must not imply unrestricted travel, trade or military access.
9. Local tactical cooperation must not silently become a formal alliance.
10. Hidden orders, infiltrators and covert intent remain knowledge-gated.
11. AI dialogue cannot mutate treaties, wars, membership, borders or official leadership.
12. Klingon, Romulan, Cardassian, Bajoran, Ferengi, Borg or Dominion-related behavior cannot be inferred from species alone.
13. Borg contact uses a nonstandard diplomacy path unless explicit local state permits negotiation.
14. Dominion subordinate/member status is temporal and must be resolved at campaign date.
15. Bajoran sovereignty and Federation presence at Deep Space 9 must remain separate authority layers.
16. Any relation anchor with uncertain chronology must remain approximate or unresolved rather than fabricated.
