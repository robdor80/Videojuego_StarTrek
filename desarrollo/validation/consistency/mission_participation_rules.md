# Mission participation validation rules

1. Mission gameplay must remain actionable below command rank.
2. Strategic mission state is decomposed into role-appropriate duty tasks.
3. Rank, clearance, position, need-to-know and live duty authority are separate access inputs.
4. A subordinate task cannot grant authority the issuer does not possess.
5. Completing a specialist task may affect a mission objective without revealing the entire mission.
6. AI may not leak redacted or command-only information through narration.
7. Cadets and trainees receive supervised but consequential gameplay tasks.
8. Player-character status grants no special authority or clearance.
9. Acting command uses the normal succession/qualification system.
10. Task history feeds debrief/evaluation only through authoritative event references.
