# Facility-assignment gameplay validation rules

1. Facility assignments are first-class career assignments.
2. Ship command authority and facility authority remain separate.
3. Facility gameplay is role-specific and knowledge-gated.
4. Local incidents originate from live state, not content demand.
5. Quiet station duty is valid gameplay.
6. NPC departments keep functioning when the player is absent.
7. Local incident consequences persist in facility, logistics, political and mission state.
8. Temporary duty and transfers use existing career/assignment pipelines.
9. Station service is not treated as inherently inferior career progression.
10. AI can roleplay facility personnel but cannot invent clearance, resources, incidents or outcomes.
