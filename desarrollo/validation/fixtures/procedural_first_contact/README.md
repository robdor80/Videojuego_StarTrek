# Procedural First Contact fixtures

These fixtures prove that discovery/materialization/contact are separate.

## Pre-warp observation

A civilization researching warp remains an **observe-only** case under normal conditions. The generator does not materialize unnecessary leaders/names simply because sensors see the planet.

## First successful warp flight

A civilization crosses the warp threshold during the campaign. The system materializes persistent civilization detail for contact preparation, but still returns:

`automatic_contact_forbidden = true`

and requires a captain decision after the First Contact brief.

Locked generated identity:
- civilization: **Raerraith**
- homeworld self-name: **Thesqolqu**
- generated species morphology: **nonhuman bilateral / fine fur**
- government: **Mesikfis**, unitary republic
- representative candidate: **Bathsatim Sailairdak**
- population estimate: **3,894,320,550**

These are game-generated fixture facts, not Star Trek canon.

## Post-warp mutual encounter

A warp civilization encounters Starfleet directly before translation exists. Contact can no longer be treated as secret, but the Universal Translator begins **untranslated** and controlled disclosure is required.

## Divided warp world

A warp-capable world has multiple sovereign states. Representatives are scoped to their own polity. First Contact with one does not create planet-wide diplomatic relations.
