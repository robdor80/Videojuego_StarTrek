# Post-Academy Professional Development

Status: **FOUNDATION v0.1 — IN PROGRESS**

## Core idea

Graduation creates a commissioned officer who knows Starfleet standards.

It does not create a finished professional.

The first years of service must remain a learning phase.

## Professional stages

### Ensign
- supervised watches;
- ship/class familiarization;
- real operational consolidation;
- basic duty qualifications;
- frequent mentor/senior review.

### Lieutenant Junior Grade
- greater autonomy;
- advanced qualifications;
- broader duty eligibility;
- growing responsibility;
- possible junior supervision.

### Lieutenant
- consolidated professional;
- trusted independent duty;
- specialization depth;
- leadership responsibilities;
- may mentor junior officers.

Later ranks continue learning through command, leadership and specialization rather than ending progression.

## Mentorship

Mentorship is a persistent professional relationship and is separate from friendship.

A mentor may:

- supervise watches;
- debrief incidents;
- correct procedure;
- explain ship/class-specific systems;
- recommend training;
- validate readiness for assessment;
- influence formal recommendations when authorized.

A mentor can have high professional trust and low personal affinity.

## Qualifications

Qualifications are evidence-based.

Typical lifecycle:

```text
theory
→ simulation
→ supervised practice
→ operational evidence
→ assessment
→ qualification
```

Example qualifications:

- Sensors Operator;
- Science Analysis;
- Bridge Watch;
- Advanced Sensor Operations;
- Away Team Science;
- Command Watch;
- class-specific familiarization.

## Class familiarization

General professional qualification survives a transfer.

A new ship class may still require familiarization.

Example:

```text
Starfleet Sensors Operator        QUALIFIED
Galaxy-class Sensor Suite         FAMILIARIZATION REQUIRED
```

## Training and life

Training consumes real calendar time and competes with:

- duty;
- rest;
- social life;
- personal interests;
- relationships.

This creates career/life trade-offs without arbitrary XP spending.

## Career loop

A player who was once mentored may later be assigned a new Ensign to supervise.

This closes the career-development loop.
