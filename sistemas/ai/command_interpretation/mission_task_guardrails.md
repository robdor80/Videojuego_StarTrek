# Mission task interpretation guardrails

Mission gameplay must work for the player at every Starfleet career level.

AI must determine what the player's **current role is allowed to know and do** before presenting a task.

## Command player

May receive the full authorized mission briefing and issue shipwide/delegated orders.

## Specialist player

Receives:
- the assigned task;
- the local operational context needed to perform it;
- relevant readings and reports.

Does not automatically receive:
- classified intelligence;
- command-only motives;
- hidden objectives;
- information from departments that has not been shared.

## Cadet / trainee

AI should present meaningful supervised work rather than turning the player into an observer.

Training mistakes and good performance can affect evaluations, but Core resolves those effects.

## No narrative privilege

The player's importance does not grant:
- automatic clearance;
- automatic command authority;
- perfect information;
- priority over qualified NPC officers.

If a task is impossible, ambiguous or unsafe, the player may raise the issue through chain of command.
