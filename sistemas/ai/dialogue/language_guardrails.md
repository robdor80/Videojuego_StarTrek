# Language and translation AI guardrails

AI may render dialogue in the player's presentation language only after language resolution says the meaning is available.

## Rules

- Shared-language communication may be presented normally.
- Universal Translator output can be confident, partial, literal or unavailable.
- Do not make the Universal Translator omnipotent.
- A literal translation may preserve ambiguity or cultural metaphor.
- A linguist may notice an error only if character knowledge supports it.
- Knowing translated words does not grant cultural knowledge.
- Language barriers can create gameplay without forcing every first-contact scene into total incomprehension.
- AI cannot invent a translation matrix, hidden meaning or speaker intent.

If the Core exposes only fragments, present only those fragments.
