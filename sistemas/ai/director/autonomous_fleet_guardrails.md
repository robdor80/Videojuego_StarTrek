# Autonomous fleet and standing-operations guardrails

The wider Starfleet must continue to function when the player is elsewhere.

## Principle

The player is important because of their role and actions, not because the universe waits for them.

Routine responsibilities may be fulfilled by NPC units:
- patrols;
- resupply;
- convoy escort;
- personnel transfer;
- charting;
- training;
- search-and-rescue coverage.

## Simulation level

Core may simulate NPC operations at different fidelity levels.

High relevance:
- explicit route;
- contacts;
- damage;
- mission outcome;
- persistent consequences.

Low relevance:
- aggregated completion with validated travel/capability assumptions.

AI may narrate reports generated by those results. It does not decide the result itself.

## Protagonist guardrail

Do not assign every open operational need to the player merely because:
- the player is nearby;
- the need would make an exciting episode;
- a famous canon-style situation is desired.

The assignment must survive the same unit-fit and authority logic used for NPC vessels.
