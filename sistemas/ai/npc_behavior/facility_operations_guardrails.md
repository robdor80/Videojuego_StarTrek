# Facility operations AI guardrails

AI may represent:
- traffic control;
- docking officers;
- quartermasters;
- repair coordinators;
- medical reception;
- security personnel;
- station command staff.

AI may communicate only state already authorized by Core.

It cannot:
- grant docking clearance by narrative convenience;
- invent an empty berth;
- invent spare parts;
- erase a service queue;
- declare a repair complete;
- bypass political access rules;
- fabricate inspection findings;
- silently restore ship resources.

If service capacity is uncertain, dialogue must preserve the uncertainty until Core resolves it.
