# Bajor — gameplay

Retain only what changes play:
- Bajor is sovereign in the DS9 baseline;
- Starfleet presence at Deep Space 9 does not erase Bajoran jurisdiction;
- Bajoran civil government, militia and religious authority are separate systems;
- occupation history affects security, diplomacy, trauma and character memory;
- wartime neutrality or non-aggression decisions are temporal state, not permanent identity.

Bajoran species never automatically grants government authority.
