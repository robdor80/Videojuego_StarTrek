# Starfleet Academy

Status: **IN PROGRESS**

Starfleet Academy is the next active research block.

## Research order

1. History and institutional role
2. Campus and facilities
3. Admissions
4. Cadet ranks and cadet life
5. Departments and specializations
6. Curriculum and courses
7. Evaluations and examinations
8. Simulations
9. Field training and training ships
10. Graduation and first assignments
11. Era profiles: Pike, Kirk, TNG/DS9/Voyager

## Gameplay principle

The Academy is not being documented as lore for its own sake.

Every Academy research sub-block must end in a usable game model.

The Academy is not a character-creation menu.

It is a playable career stage in which the player can:
- enter as a cadet,
- train,
- choose or develop a specialization,
- be evaluated,
- build relationships and reputation,
- experience simulations and field exercises,
- graduate,
- receive a first assignment.

## Canon/campaign rule

Academy canon provides the institution and starting context.

The player does not replay Academy scenes from television or films.


## Required gameplay outputs

Academy research must progressively define:

- `CadetState`
- admission flow
- Academy location graph
- timetable / activity model
- specialization path
- course and exam model
- simulation model
- evaluation model
- relationship/reputation hooks
- disciplinary hooks
- field-training hooks
- graduation criteria
- first-assignment generation
- era-specific overrides

Lore that does not affect one of these systems remains supporting documentation, not the main deliverable.
