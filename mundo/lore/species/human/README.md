# Humanos — gameplay

Humano is the comparative humanoid baseline, not a default personality.

Gameplay consequences:
- no universal species power or flat stat bonus;
- Earth upbringing is separate from Human species;
- Human colonies may have different cultures, laws and social assumptions;
- Human does not imply Federation citizenship;
- medical treatment uses Human baseline unless individual state says otherwise.

Primary cross-check: `MA_HUMAN`.
