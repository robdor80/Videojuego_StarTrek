# Characters

Characters are persistent live entities, not disposable dialogue generators.

## Separation

```text
IDENTITY
≠ SPECIES
≠ CULTURE
≠ CITIZENSHIP
≠ FACTION
≠ RANK
≠ POSITION
≠ PERSONALITY
≠ KNOWLEDGE
≠ RELATIONSHIP
≠ REPUTATION
```

## Campaign rule

A canonical character is initialized from history only up to campaign start.

After that:

```text
LIVE CAMPAIGN HISTORY > FUTURE SCREEN CANON
```

A generated NPC becomes persistent once introduced and can accumulate:
- memories;
- relationships;
- reputation;
- injuries;
- promotions/transfers;
- knowledge;
- grudges;
- favors;
- commendations;
- discipline;
- personal goals.

## AI boundary

AI portrays the character.

Core owns:
- identity;
- location;
- knowledge;
- memory;
- relationships;
- authority;
- medical state;
- factual outcomes.
