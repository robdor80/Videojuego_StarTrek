# Starship refit consistency rules

1. Class baseline, era configuration, refit package and individual ship state remain separate.
2. A completed refit modifies only the individual ship/configuration that received it.
3. Refit technology cannot exceed era availability without explicit special/experimental provenance.
4. Major structural/interior refits require topology/configuration overrides.
5. Individual-only modifications are never generalized to a class.
6. Refit work consumes campaign time, facility capacity, parts and specialist labor where required.
7. Facility type alone does not guarantee refit capability.
8. Partial or blocked refits do not grant the full final capability set.
9. Test phases can reveal failures that require follow-up work.
10. Canonical pre-campaign refits initialize history only; future canonical refits do not auto-apply after campaign start.
11. Refit completion appends a persistent ship-history event.
12. AI may explain or roleplay refit work but cannot grant technology, finish work or alter configuration directly.
