# Campaign astrography initialization rules

1. Physical existence, political control and chart knowledge load as separate layers.
2. A later-era chart must never leak automatically into an earlier campaign.
3. A physical node may exist in World Truth while remaining absent from player charts.
4. Bajoran wormhole strategic knowledge is date/discovery gated.
5. Voyager/Delta Quadrant knowledge is contextual and may remain local to a ship or delayed by communications.
6. Sparse anchor catalogues are intentional; absence is not nonexistence.
7. Exact coordinates must not be invented to make a map look complete.
8. Political region labels are organizational unless a source establishes a precise border.
9. Wormholes and other special traversal links use dedicated traversal mechanics.
10. Campaign discoveries may reveal locations earlier than screen history; live campaign state then becomes authoritative.
