# Dominion/Borg species-state consistency rules

1. Borg drone is an assimilated character state, not a species replacement.
2. Origin species persists through Borg assimilation for physiology and medical logic.
3. Assimilation is staged and Core-owned.
4. Changeling form copying never copies memories, credentials, authority or relationships.
5. Changeling detection requires evidence.
6. Jem'Hadar Ketracel-white dependency is medical/logistics state, not narrative flavor.
7. Exact dose timing or deprivation outcome is never invented.
8. Vorta/Jem'Hadar engineered loyalty context does not replace individual character state.
9. Founder political authority, Vorta command authority and Jem'Hadar obedience are resolved through live faction/command state.
10. AI cannot convert collective/faction knowledge into omniscient character knowledge.
