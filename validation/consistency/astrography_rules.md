# Astrography gameplay validation rules

1. Physical location and political ownership are separate data.
2. Border state is temporal and must resolve by campaign date.
3. Future canonical border changes never auto-apply after campaign start.
4. Every nontrivial coordinate/distance must carry source and confidence.
5. Licensed-reference maps do not become SCREEN_CANON by ingestion.
6. Map-derived and warp-time-derived distances remain visibly lower-confidence than direct screen statements or real astronomy.
7. Unknown Z coordinates stay unknown; do not flatten the galaxy by convenience.
8. The 20 ly sector cube from Star Charts is a reference scheme, not an immutable universal law.
9. Sector identifiers must not be used as a substitute for coordinates when the source does not establish geometry.
10. Wormholes, transwarp networks and subspace corridors are traversal edges with special rules, not ordinary straight-line distance.
11. Route planning must account for current ship capability, damage, power and local conditions.
12. Political borders can change route legality without changing physical distance.
13. AI cannot expose hidden hazards, secret installations or unexplored locations without a valid knowledge path.
14. A route ETA should be a range unless the simulation has sufficient precision.
15. Contradictory sources must coexist as hypotheses until a project decision resolves them.
