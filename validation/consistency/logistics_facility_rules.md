# Logistics and facility consistency rules

1. Star Trek post-scarcity technology does not eliminate operational logistics.
2. Resource state is multidimensional; do not reduce all endurance to one fuel value.
3. Exact canonical quantities must not be invented when sources do not establish them.
4. Facility permanent capability and current service availability are separate.
5. A starbase or station does not automatically provide every service.
6. Damage, staffing, inventory, political access and service queues affect support availability.
7. Resupply and repair consume operational time.
8. Partial resupply and partial repair are valid outcomes.
9. Mission feasibility must consider endurance, reserves and reachable support.
10. Replicator/synthesizer capability cannot silently fabricate unsupported specialized components, ordnance or unlimited repair resources.
11. NPC and player units use the same logistics principles.
12. AI may report logistics state but cannot create inventory, parts, berths or support capacity.
