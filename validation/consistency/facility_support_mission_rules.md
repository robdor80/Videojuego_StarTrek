# Facility support and mission-feasibility rules

1. Mission feasibility checks outbound, on-station and return/recovery requirements.
2. Support facilities count only if reachable, politically accessible and operationally capable.
3. Facility queues and delays can affect mission windows.
4. Exact support capacity is never invented merely to produce a precise ETA.
5. A facility may provide some services while others are disabled.
6. Service completion mutates only the resources/systems actually supported.
7. Repair never exceeds class/configuration design limits.
8. Logistics shortages must exist in live state or standing requirements before generating a mission.
9. NPC logistics traffic may satisfy routine needs without player involvement.
10. A player may accept logistics risk where command authority permits; the risk decision is persistent evidence.
11. No universal instant 'full repair/full resupply' action exists.
12. AI cannot fabricate stock, parts, queue position or repair capacity.
