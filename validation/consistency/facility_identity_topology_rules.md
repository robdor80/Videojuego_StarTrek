# Facility identity and topology consistency rules

1. Facility type, design/class, individual facility, owner and operator remain separate.
2. Ownership changes do not rewrite physical design identity.
3. Blueprint-derived geometry is reference evidence, not automatic SCREEN_CANON.
4. Unknown geometry is not invented merely to complete a location graph.
5. Baseline topology and live interior state are separate.
6. Damage/security/pressure state may block a connection without deleting it from baseline topology.
7. Access control and physical connectivity are separate.
8. Individual/refit/configuration differences may override baseline design topology.
9. Service capability may exist without a known precise room or bay.
10. Concrete service-node locations require source support.
11. NPC and player traversal use the same authoritative live topology state.
12. AI may describe known/interpreted spaces but cannot invent rooms, connections or active services.
