# Species gameplay validation rules

A species profile is valid only if all checks pass.

1. No encyclopedia-only profile: every retained fact must support an action, constraint, medical rule, perception rule, social interpretation or character-creation decision.
2. Species != culture: biological and cultural data stay separate.
3. Species != personality: no mandatory temperament, morality or profession.
4. Species != citizenship: homeworld or Federation membership cannot be inferred from anatomy.
5. No flat fantasy bonuses: capabilities are contextual and resolved by Core.
6. Individual variation: trainable or variable capabilities require per-character state.
7. Knowledge gates: private biology, medical states and uncommon cultural knowledge remain hidden unless the observer has a valid source of knowledge.
8. No AI mutation: dialogue cannot create telepathy, illness, cultural authority or physiological effects.
9. No automatic hostility: Tellarite argument and Andorian directness require context.
10. Vulcan emotion rule: emotional control is not emotional absence.
11. Hybrid safety: hybrid characters require explicit lineage/medical data; do not average parent-species traits automatically.
12. Era safety: later cultural/political facts are not backfilled into Pike/Kirk without temporal evidence.
