# Standing operations and autonomous fleet validation rules

1. Routine missions may originate from standing operational requirements without a crisis event.
2. A standing requirement generates work only when due, uncovered or triggered by live state.
3. NPC units and player units share the same basic capability/readiness eligibility logic.
4. The player is not automatically preferred over a better-fit NPC unit.
5. NPC routine operations may be simulated at reduced fidelity when no player-relevant decision depends on detail.
6. Reduced-fidelity simulation still produces authoritative outcomes and relevant state changes.
7. Current mission priority, repair status, crew readiness and legal access affect unit availability.
8. Destroyed, missing or otherwise unavailable units cannot be selected.
9. Standing schedules do not auto-create future canon events.
10. Political or wartime changes may suspend, replace or reprioritize routine requirements.
11. AI can present reports from autonomous operations but cannot choose winners, fabricate outcomes or mutate state.
