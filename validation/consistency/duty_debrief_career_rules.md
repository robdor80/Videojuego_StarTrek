# Duty, handoff and debrief validation rules

1. Rank, permanent position, current duty station and current watch authority remain separate.
2. Watch duration and staffing are configuration/live-state data, not universal canon constants.
3. Emergency succession may alter current authority without rewriting rank.
4. Handoff contains known information only.
5. Unresolved operational items persist across watch changes.
6. Mission briefing distinguishes confirmed facts, assessments and unknowns.
7. Debrief is derived from authoritative events.
8. Mission outcome and individual performance are not the same value.
9. A successful mission can produce negative conduct evidence; a failed mission can produce positive performance evidence.
10. Mission evidence feeds evaluation and career pipelines without XP-based automatic promotion.
11. AI may narrate briefings/debriefings but may not fabricate evidence, scores, commendations or discipline.
12. Career consequences require the appropriate authority/review pipeline.
