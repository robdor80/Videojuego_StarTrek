# Federation political-state validation

The Federation political model must satisfy all of the following:

1. **Species is not citizenship.** Species data cannot automatically assign Federation membership, diplomatic authority or political loyalty.
2. **World is not government.** A location record and its active political entity are separate.
3. **Federation is not Starfleet.** Starfleet may execute federal policy but does not replace the civilian government.
4. **Temporal membership.** Membership and external relations are resolved at campaign date and then become live World State.
5. **No future destiny.** Canon events after campaign start are never auto-applied.
6. **No invented constitutional precision.** Missing election terms, seat formulas, accession procedures or fiscal mechanics remain unknown unless evidence or an explicit GAME_ADDITION is introduced.
7. **Late-era scale is not retroactive.** The 2373 scale figure cannot populate Pike/Kirk membership counts.
8. **AI cannot mutate state.** Diplomatic dialogue can propose, threaten, accept or refuse; Core resolves and records authoritative consequences.
