# Facility traffic and transfer consistency rules

1. Inbound, holding, approach, docked and departing states remain distinct.
2. Docking clearance, berth assignment and service approval are separate.
3. Traffic saturation changes delay or priority but does not create extra physical capacity.
4. Visiting ships remain independent entities with their own command and live state.
5. Transfers require explicit source, destination and manifest or resource references.
6. Transfer jobs update the relevant authoritative systems.
7. Unknown contacts remain knowledge-gated in traffic control.
8. NPC traffic continues independently of player presence.
9. Routine traffic does not become a mission unless live conditions create an operational need.
10. AI may roleplay traffic control but cannot invent clearance, berths, manifests or traffic conflicts.
