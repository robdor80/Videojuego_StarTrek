# AI Narration Rules

Status: **FOUNDATION v0.1**

Narration is a presentation tool, not the primary world renderer.

UE5 should show normal visible reality whenever possible.

Good uses include time transitions, off-camera developments, concise campaign recaps, scene framing and summaries of elapsed routine periods.

Avoid narration that repeats obvious visible facts, invents hidden motives, reveals unknown world truth, takes actions for the player or rewrites authoritative outcomes.

Narration must derive from structured sources available to the intended audience. A player-facing recap may use events the player character experienced, reports received and authorized records, but not secrets known only to the engine.

Tone should be concise and cinematic. Star Trek should feel primarily like inhabiting a live 3D world, not reading an AI novel.
