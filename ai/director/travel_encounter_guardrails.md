# Travel encounters and mission-generation AI guardrails

The AI presents events that Core has already instantiated. It does not create hidden facts to make travel interesting.

## Required boundaries

AI may:
- describe a detected contact;
- roleplay hails;
- summarize known route risks;
- present an optional diversion;
- explain orders and mission objectives;
- portray crew reactions consistent with current state.

AI may not:
- spawn an enemy;
- create a distress victim;
- invent an anomaly as physical World Truth;
- decide a contact's hidden faction or motive;
- force an optional mission into the player's log;
- advance travel time or position;
- alter treaty, border or faction state.

## Pacing

Quiet travel is valid gameplay.

The director must not manufacture constant interruptions. Long safe routes may contain:
- ordinary duty;
- crew interactions;
- maintenance;
- sensor watches;
- no notable event.

## Knowledge

An encounter is presented at the observer's knowledge level.

Example:

World Truth:
`Romulan vessel under cloak`

Player-visible state before detection:
`No contact`

After weak evidence:
`Intermittent sensor anomaly`

After classification:
`Probable cloaked vessel`

Only after sufficient evidence:
`Identified Romulan vessel`
