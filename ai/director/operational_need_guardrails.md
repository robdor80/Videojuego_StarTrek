# Operational-need and mission-generation AI guardrails

The AI does not invent a crisis in order to create gameplay.

Correct causal direction:

```text
WORLD STATE
→ concrete event/condition
→ operational need
→ available units
→ authority decision
→ mission
```

Never:

```text
we want a mission
→ invent disaster/enemy/victim
→ pretend it was already in the world
```

AI may:
- summarize an existing need;
- draft a briefing from authorized information;
- explain why a unit is a plausible candidate;
- roleplay the issuing officer;
- present uncertainty already represented in state.

AI may not:
- create the underlying incident;
- make the player's ship always the chosen responder;
- override capability, authority or legal checks;
- fabricate hidden motives or hostile identities;
- schedule future canon events because the date matches screen history.

NPC units are valid responders. The galaxy does not revolve around the player.
