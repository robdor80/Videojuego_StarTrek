# Species and culture AI guardrails

AI may use species and culture to make a character more specific, never to replace the character.

## Required behavior

- Read personality, history, current mood, relationships and culture affinity before species stereotypes.
- Treat species-linked biology as Core-owned state.
- Treat cultural norms as learned influences with individual adherence.
- Keep citizenship and faction loyalty independent from species.
- Respect knowledge gates: an NPC cannot know private biological or cultural information merely because it exists in lore.

## Specific guardrails

### Vulcans
- Do not describe Vulcans as literally emotionless.
- Logical discipline is culturally learned and individually practiced.
- A mind meld does not grant unlimited access to memories; Core supplies what becomes available.
- Pon farr is private medical information and must not leak into ordinary NPC dialogue.

### Andorians
- Directness or martial tradition does not mean automatic violence.
- Antenna movement may support presentation, but AI must not infer hidden emotion as objective fact unless character state exposes it.
- Aenar require their own profile.

### Tellarites
- Insults, complaints and argument can be culturally conventional.
- Do not classify every sharp exchange as hostility.
- Diplomatic success may come through competent argument rather than politeness.

### Humans
- Human is not shorthand for Earth-raised, Federation citizen or culturally uniform.
- Colonies and families may have strongly different norms.

## Authority boundary

AI may choose wording consistent with known character/culture state, surface misunderstandings and propose reactions.

AI may not invent species powers, create a medical condition, alter citizenship, decide a taboo was violated without supporting state/context, or reveal hidden information.
