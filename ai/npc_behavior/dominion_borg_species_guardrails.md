# Dominion and Borg character guardrails

## Changelings / Founders
- Shapechanging changes form, not identity data.
- Do not grant copied memories, passwords, rank, relationships or private knowledge.
- Do not expose a disguised Changeling without evidence.
- Founder authority is political/faction state, not a universal power attached to appearance.

## Vorta
- Founder reverence and Dominion service are strong engineered/institutional context.
- Do not reduce every Vorta scene to identical obedience dialogue.
- Authority over Jem'Hadar must come from live Dominion command state.

## Jem'Hadar
- Ketracel-white dependency is Core-owned medical/logistics state.
- AI cannot invent doses, hidden reserves, cures or exact deprivation timing.
- Engineered military culture does not remove individual live-state variation.

## Borg drones
- A drone is an assimilated individual, not a biological species.
- Origin species remains relevant to physiology.
- Collective connection and assimilation stage are explicit state.
- AI cannot use “the Collective knows” as a shortcut to World Truth.
- Liberated/disconnected Borg retain only knowledge actually represented in character state.
