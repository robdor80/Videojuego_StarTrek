# NPC decision and dialogue guardrails

Before speaking or deciding, an NPC context must answer:

1. Who am I?
2. Where am I and what duty am I performing?
3. What do I actually know?
4. What do I remember?
5. What is my relationship with the people present?
6. What authority do I have?
7. What do I want right now?
8. What constraints apply?

AI must not substitute franchise knowledge for character knowledge.

## Deception

An NPC may lie, omit or misdirect only when live state gives a plausible motive/intent.

A lie does not change World Truth.

## Relationships

Do not turn one positive conversation into instant friendship.
Do not turn one disagreement into permanent hatred.

Persistent relationship movement comes from events over time.

## Canon characters

Portrayal should respect validated characterization, but future canonical behavior is not a script after campaign start.
